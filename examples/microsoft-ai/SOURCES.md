# Microsoft AI stewardship — source register

**Observation/retrieval date: 4 October 2026.** Sources were inspected for the passages identified below. The full Microsoft transparency-report PDF, every product policy, independent assurance and current jurisdictional legal requirements are not comprehensively reviewed in this sample.

## S1 — Responsible-AI disclosure

Microsoft, [Responsible AI Transparency Report 2026 — public HTML overview](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/corporate-responsibility/topics/responsible-ai/reports/transparency-report/).

Locators: **Scaling adaptable governance**, **Meeting the agentic moment**, **Lessons from deploying AI**. Use: company descriptions of governance, developer/deployer responsibilities and deployment review. Classification: **issuer statement**, not independent evidence of control effectiveness or complete incident reporting. The comparison with financially material outcomes is HHFinAi's inference and proposed research design.

## S2 — Engineering disclosure

Steve Solomon, Microsoft, [Sustainable by design: Next-generation datacenters consume zero water for cooling](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-cloud/blog/2024/12/09/sustainable-by-design-next-generation-datacenters-consume-zero-water-for-cooling/), **9 December 2024**.

Locators: **Mitigating energy impacts**, **Pilot projects and implementation**, and WUE definition/footnote. Use: the disclosed engineering trade-off and measurement questions. Design expectations and future schedules remain dated company statements. Zero direct cooling-water consumption is not zero total-site or indirect water use. Consumption/withdrawal wording should be reconciled before model calibration.
