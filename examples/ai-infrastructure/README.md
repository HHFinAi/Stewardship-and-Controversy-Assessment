# AI infrastructure: turn financial uncertainty into verifiable requests

**Prepared 16 October 2026 | Proposed engagement only | NEEDS_DATA.** No request has been sent, deadline agreed, issuer response received or outcome observed. AI assistance was used; independent human review and workflow approval are not recorded.

The [original financial case](https://github.com/HHFinAi/Sustainability-to-Financial-Materiality-Analysis/tree/main/examples/ai-infrastructure) tests whether earlier capacity can justify additional power infrastructure. Its project economics are illustrative. The purpose of this handoff is to identify evidence that would replace those assumptions, not to ask Microsoft to meet the model's numerical hurdles.

## Five proposed requests

| ID | Evidence requested | What would count as progress | Financial consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI01 | Phase-specific ownership, utility/customer commitments, delivery milestones, permits and cancellation obligations | Reconciled phase register and appropriately scoped, non-confidential contract or assurance evidence | Replace commissioning, utilization, power-price and capital-recovery assumptions |
| AI02 | Economic equipment life, replacement spending, redeployment and resale; separate book and tax lives | Cohort-level reconciliation with dated assumptions and sensitivity to shorter useful service | Recalculate replacement cash flows and residual values |
| AI03 | Actual power architecture, delivery dependencies, backup arrangements and cost allocation | Engineering/contract boundary showing who pays for capacity, fuel, standby service and outages | Compare grid, dedicated generation and flexible-workload routes on consistent costs |
| AI04 | Phase-level water source, withdrawals/consumption, seasonal constraints, land disturbance and mitigation outcomes | Scoped measurements and applicable permission/mitigation evidence, with baseline and period | Change capex, opex or operating availability only where a supported mechanism exists |
| AI05 | Operational-technology responsibility, tested recovery objectives, service credits and liability allocation | Non-sensitive assurance or aggregate test evidence with coverage and limitations | Replace conditional outage severity and recoverability inputs; frequency remains unknown without evidence |

The [machine-readable ledger](milestones.json) links each request to primary-source IDs and the financial case's open issues. Requested evidence may require a confidential channel; do not publish confidential contracts, security architecture, vulnerabilities or sensitive ecological coordinates.

## Decision and escalation rules

An announcement, meeting or document receipt is an intermediate event. Mark a criterion met only after dated evidence addresses its scope and an accountable reviewer records the rationale. Partial coverage stays partial. No missing public disclosure is treated as misconduct or proof that a control is absent.

If a material assumption remains unsupported, retain `NEEDS_DATA` and keep the affected scenario illustrative. A future authorized engagement should set owners, agreed dates and escalation steps against the investment mandate. This ledger creates no scheduled action, automatic vote or divestment trigger.
